Privacy Policy

Mazarko (MAZAR.KO) app — Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy explains what data the Mazarko (MAZAR.KO) app and its website collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy. The app is provided by Ahmed Ali ("we", "us").

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, phone number, and sign-in credentials.
  • Farm data: flock information and daily records (mortality, feed, weight, temperature, eggs, notes), vaccinations, treatments, daily tasks, and optional photos you attach to records.
  • Team data: email addresses of people you invite to help manage your farm, and their roles and permissions.
  • Marketplace data: listings you post (description, price, contact phone number), orders, and ratings.
  • Identity verification (for sellers in the marketplace): national ID number and photos of the front and back of the ID card.
  • Payment data: we do not store your bank card details. Payments are processed by our payment provider (Paymob). We keep only a record of the transaction (purpose, amount, status, reference number).
  • AI assistant: the questions and messages you send to the assistant, and the credits used.
  • Usage and technical data: your activity in the app (such as the daily streak counter and referral code), device type, and server logs needed for operation and security.

Permissions: the app may ask for access to the camera or photo gallery (to upload record photos or ID photos), internet access, and notifications if you enable them. You can revoke any permission in your device settings at any time.

2. How we use your data

  • Create your account and run the app's features: flock management, reports, forecasts, and alerts.
  • Operate the marketplace: show your listings and enable contact between sellers and buyers.
  • Verify sellers' identity to protect users from fraud.
  • Process payments (Pro subscription, merchant account fee, unlocking a listing's contact number).
  • Provide AI assistant answers and improve service quality.
  • Contact you about your account or support, and protect the app against abuse.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only in these cases:

  • Service providers: Paymob (payment processing), Google (Gemini AI services), and the cloud hosting provider our servers run on (Render). They process data on our behalf and only to provide the service.
  • Other users: your listing's public details are visible to others, but your phone number is shown only to you and to users who paid to unlock that listing's contact number.
  • Your team members: they can see your flock data according to the permissions you give them.
  • Legal authorities: when required by law or a court order, or to protect our rights and users' safety.

4. Artificial intelligence

Your questions and some relevant flock data may be sent to an external AI service (Google Gemini) to generate answers and analyses. Please do not enter unnecessary sensitive information (such as your national ID number) in the chat. AI answers are advisory only and are not a substitute for a veterinarian or other professional.

5. Identity verification

We collect the national ID number and ID card photos once, only to verify a seller's identity. They are stored on our servers and are visible only to the account owner and to authorized system administrators when review is needed. We never use them for marketing.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active or as long as needed to provide the service. You can request deletion of your account and data yourself at https://delete-account-siteee.onrender.com (works even if the app is not installed), or by emailing abdelghanya2977@gmail.com. We will delete it within a reasonable time, except for data we must keep by law (such as payment records) or to prevent fraud.

7. Data security

We transmit data over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restrict access to sensitive data to people who need it. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your sign-in details confidential.

8. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, withdraw your consent, and object to certain processing, as permitted by law. Send your request to abdelghanya2977@gmail.com. We aim to comply with Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020.

9. Children

The app is not directed to anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect children's data. If we learn that we have collected a child's data, we will delete it promptly.

10. International data transfers

Your data may be stored or processed on servers outside Egypt by our providers. We choose providers that apply appropriate safeguards to protect data.

11. External links

The app may link to other sites or services (such as the payment page). We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so we recommend reading their policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The new version will be posted here with its update date, and we will notify you in the app if the change is significant.
